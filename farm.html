Our Story

A working farm,
not a factory.

We started Tyleigh Farm with a handful of hens and a stubborn belief that food tastes better when it's grown with care. A decade later we're still a family operation — moving cattle at dawn, gathering eggs by hand, and picking tomatoes the same morning you take them home.

Everything we raise stays close to home, sold straight from our store and at the Saturday market. When you buy from us, you know exactly where your food came from and who stood in the field to grow it.

🌱

Regenerative by habit

We rotate our animals daily so the land gets richer every season — deeper roots, healthier soil, more life.

☀️

Raised outdoors

Sunshine, grass, and fresh air. Our animals live the way they were meant to, and you can taste the difference.

CSA Shares

A season of the farm, delivered to your table.

Community Supported Agriculture means you sign on for the season and we set aside the best of every harvest for you. Pick up at the farm or the Saturday market — no contracts, cancel anytime.

Shares run in 12-week seasons. First pickup begins the week after you sign up.
