a letter from the farm

Come sit a while

A few years back we brought home one dun-colored cow named Nestle, mostly because we couldn't stop thinking about her. She followed us along the fence like a big shaggy dog, and that was that — we were Highland people.

These days the fold has grown, but the heart of it hasn't. We move the cattle at dawn, gather eggs by hand, and know every animal by name and by temperament. When one of ours goes to a new home, we want it to be a home as glad to have them as we've been.

So look around, meet the girls, read their stories. And when you find one you can't stop thinking about — the way we couldn't stop thinking about Nestle — write to us. We'd love that.

— the Wallace family
