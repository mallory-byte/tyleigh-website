How to reserve

There's nothing complicated here, and no pressure at any step. This is how a Highland goes from our pasture to yours.

  1. Reach out and say hello

    Send a note through the inquiry form, or call. Tell us a little about your place and what you're hoping for — a gentle pasture companion, a foundation heifer, a whole small fold. We'll talk it through honestly and help you find the right match, even if that match isn't ready yet.

  2. A deposit holds your animal

    When you've found the one, a deposit reserves them and takes them off the available page. The deposit goes toward the purchase and is what lets us say no to everyone else. We'll put the particulars in a simple written agreement so we're both clear.

  3. We keep you posted

    Between reserving and coming home, we send photos and updates as your animal grows. If it's a calf still on its mother, we'll raise it out to weaning and get it gentle and halter-broke before it travels.

  4. Pickup or transport, sorted together

    You're welcome to come with a trailer and meet the whole fold when you collect them — we love that. If you're farther off, we'll help arrange a reputable livestock hauler and get you a fair quote. We handle the transfer of registration papers into your name as part of the sale.

  5. Welcome to the fold

    Once they're home we don't disappear. Call us with questions any time — about feeding, hooves, calving, anything. When you buy from a small farm you're buying the farmer's phone number too.
