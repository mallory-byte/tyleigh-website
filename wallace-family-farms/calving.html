the best time of year

Calving season

There's no better week on this farm than the one the calves arrive. Small, shaggy, wobbling around on brand-new legs — it's the whole reason, right there in the grass.

When they come

Our calves arrive in late winter, most of them clustered around mid-February. Highland calves are small and vigorous, up and nursing almost at once, and we pull the calving pasture close to the house so we can keep watch through the cold nights. By spring the field is full of them.

counting down to our next calves

How the list works

We keep a simple waitlist, in order, of folks hoping for a calf. As the season nears and we know who's carrying what, we work down the list and match people to calves — temperament and color preferences and all. There's no cost to be on it and no obligation when your turn comes.

What to expect

  • We'll reach out with photos as calves hit the ground and let you know what's coming.
  • Calves stay on their mothers until they're naturally weaned — never rushed.
  • Before they travel, we gentle them and get them started on a halter.
  • Then it's the happy business of sorting pickup and welcoming them to your place.
The gentlest calves tend to be spoken for first. If your heart is set on one, the time to say so is before they're born.
