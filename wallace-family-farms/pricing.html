Pricing & purchasing

We price our animals fairly and we're happy to explain how. A registered Highland isn't a commodity with a sticker — its value comes from a few real things, and knowing them helps you buy well, from us or anyone.

What shapes the price

  • Bloodline & registration. Recorded parentage with strong, proven lines is worth more — and means your animal's calves can be registered too.
  • Temperament. A halter-broke, people-friendly animal took hundreds of quiet hours to make. That gentleness is part of what you're buying.
  • Sex & purpose. A foundation-quality heifer or a proven herd sire carries breeding value a steer companion doesn't.
  • Color & conformation. Good structure, correct horns, and desirable coloring all factor in — though we'd take temperament over color any day.
  • Age & training. Weaned, halter-broke, and started animals cost more than a calf still on its mother, because the work is already done.

What a buyer gets from us

  • An animal registered with the American Highland Cattle Association, with papers transferred into your name.
  • An honest account of temperament and health — the good and anything you should know.
  • Gentling and halter-breaking before travel, whenever the animal's age allows.
  • Help arranging pickup or transport at a fair rate.
  • Our phone number, for as long as you own them.
We'd rather place an animal in the right home at a fair price than the highest one. We're playing a long game, and so are our cattle.

A note on figures

Prices vary with everything above, so we quote each animal individually rather than post a rate card. When something is available, its price is listed on its page or shared the moment you ask. No haggling games, no hidden fees.
